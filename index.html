Delivery

We bring the box to you.

  1. We deliver your own FreshScent Box™ to your home, along with personal laundry bags and receipts. No rent, no setup fee.
  2. Place your garments in your bag, fill out the receipt, and drop it in your box anytime it’s convenient.
  3. Ready for pickup? Text or call (555) 010-2024, or email hello@freshscentbox.com: “Please pick up my dry cleaning. My ID number is [your ID].”
Pickup

We clean it and bring it home.

  1. We’ll pick up your dry cleaning within 2–6 hours of your request.
  2. Dropped it off after 1am from the movies, or 4am from a party? We’ll pick it up the next day.
  3. Either way, we’ll let you know within 48 hours that your dry cleaning is ready, then deliver it straight back to your door.
